## Block 2 — Content Block 006

**Heading:** Key benefits

**Description:**

Independent TAB closes the gap between installed plant and design performance—before critical loads are applied.

**List items:**

- **Thermal stability** — Verifies chilled air or water reaches server intakes and maintains a stable environment under load.
- **Energy efficiency** — Balancing matches delivery to real load so plant runs at the lowest safe settings, reducing energy and cost.
- **Equipment protection** — TAB reports support warranty validation and confirm operation within manufacturer limits.
- **Design validation** — Establishes baseline performance at handover and confirms the original design intent.

## Block 3 — Content Section Two

**Heading:** The commissioning workflow

**Intro (18px):**

Our workflow is structured to catch design and installation issues early, then prove performance under realistic conditions before final certification.

**Detail (16px):**

- **Initial design review** — Feasibility input so cooling systems can be flushed, balanced, and commissioned as intended.  
- **Feasibility study** — Engineers review design documents against practical commissioning requirements.  
- **Site inspection** — Physical installation is checked against schematics; dampers and valves must be accessible.  
- **Proportional balancing** — Air and water flows are adjusted systematically to design setpoints across all plant.  
- **Performance testing** — Systems are tested under simulated load to verify thermal stability and pressure control.  
- **Discrepancy reporting** — Deviations from design intent are recorded and tracked to resolution.  
- **Final certification** — A comprehensive TAB report confirms data hall cooling capacity and efficiency.
