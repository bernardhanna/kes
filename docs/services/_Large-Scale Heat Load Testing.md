## Block 2 — Content Block 006

**Heading:** Essential validation for data centres

**Description:**

Staged thermal testing proves cooling and power paths under controlled load—without risking production equipment.

**List items:**

- **Design intent verification** — Confirms the cooling system can handle maximum designed thermal load before servers are installed.
- **Real-world simulation** — Heater banks mimic heat output and airflow patterns of high-density racks.
- **Hotspot identification** — Highlights stagnant air or insufficient cooling so floor tiles and containment can be adjusted.
- **Redundancy testing** — Verifies remaining plant maintains setpoint during simulated mechanical failure.
- **Electrical stress testing** — Confirms UPS, generators, and distribution remain stable at full load.
- **Performance baselines** — Delivers data to support SLAs for uptime and operational acceptance.

## Block 3 — Content Section Two

**Heading:** Deployment and capacity

**Intro (18px):**

We manage the full test environment—from load bank positioning and sensor integration to incremental load steps that map the cooling response curve.

**Detail (16px):**

Thermal stress is applied in stages (typically 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of target load) so engineers can observe plant response, adjust controls, and sign off each milestone before the next increment. Real-time monitoring captures temperatures, flows, and alarms throughout.
